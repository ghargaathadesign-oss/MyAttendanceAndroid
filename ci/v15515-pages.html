Privacy Policy

Updated 14 October 2026

Your information

My Attendance uses your verified login to keep each account separate. Attendance, salary, leave settings and profile details are stored on your device. Documents are encrypted with your device security.

Cloud backup

When you choose Backup, an encrypted copy is stored in your account’s Firebase cloud storage. Restore requires your device verification. Other users cannot access your backup.

Help chats

Messages you send in Help, your account name and email are stored in Firebase cloud storage so support can read and reply. Only your verified account and the support account can access your conversation. Chat messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Do not share passwords, PINs or sensitive documents.

Notifications and permissions

Firebase uses a device token to deliver app updates. Notification permission enables alerts and reminders. File access is used only when you choose a file or export a document. Device security is used for app lock, backup and restore.

Your choices

You can change language, disable notifications, export your records, clear your help chat, or delete your account from Settings. Delete Profile removes your local account data and cloud backup; use Clear chat first to remove your support conversation.

Contact support

Open Help & Support in Settings to ask a question or request help with your data.

Help & Support

How can we help?

Get help with attendance, leave balance, salary, backup or your account.

How does paid leave work?

Annual allowance is divided by 12. Enter unused leave from earlier months when saving Leave Setup. Unused monthly leave carries forward.

How do I protect my data?

Use Backup before changing devices. Keep your device lock enabled and restore with the same verified account.

Why is my salary an estimate?

Salary uses recorded attendance, calendar days, overtime and saved adjustments. Review your records and salary settings.
