Attendance & time
How do I record my working hours?

Tap Clock In when you arrive and Clock Out when you leave. Open Attendance to review or edit a record.

Leave balance
How does paid leave work?

Annual allowance is divided by 12. Enter unused leave from earlier months when saving Leave Setup. Unused monthly leave carries forward.

Salary
Why is my salary an estimate?

Salary uses recorded attendance, calendar days, overtime and saved adjustments. Review your records and salary settings.

Backup & account
How do I protect my data?

Use Backup before changing devices. Keep your device lock enabled and restore with the same verified account.
